## 2. Data we process

### Browsing the website

- **Usage and performance metrics**: the website uses Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights, which record, in aggregate, the pages visited, the referring website, the country, the device and browser type and performance metrics. These tools **don't use cookies** and don't identify you individually.
- **Access logs**: the hosting provider records technical data about requests, such as IP address, date and time, page accessed and browser, for the website's security and operation.
- **Theme preference**: your choice between the light and dark theme is stored only in your browser (local storage) and isn't sent to the website.

### Contact

When you get in touch by email or WhatsApp, we process the data you send, such as your name, email, phone number and the content of the message.

### Purchases of digital products

Payments are processed by **AbacatePay**, which collects the data needed for the charge, such as name, email, phone number and CPF or CNPJ (Brazilian tax IDs). We receive the information needed to identify the purchase, grant access to the product, provide support and meet tax obligations. We don't store card or bank account data.

### Use of products and services

- **Digital products**: each tool has a privacy policy of its own, published on its page, describing the data it processes. See the [AI Summarizer](/tool/ai-summarizer/privacy) one.
- **Professional services**: we process the data needed for the proposal, the contract and the performance of the service. Third-party data we access in the client's systems is processed only according to the client's instructions and the contract.

### MCP server

Requests to the website's MCP server (`/api/mcp`) only generate the same technical access logs described above.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases

The legal bases are those of article 7 of the LGPD:

- **Operation, security and improvement of the website**: legitimate interest.
- **Replying to contacts and quote requests**: preliminary procedures for a contract and legitimate interest.
- **Processing purchases, granting access and providing support**: performance of a contract.
- **Issuing tax documents and keeping records**: compliance with a legal obligation.
- **Providing professional services**: performance of a contract.

- **Access logs**: for the 6 months required by article 15 of the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Marco Civil da Internet).
- **Purchase data and tax documents**: for the periods required by tax law.

After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 8. Your rights

- data portability;
- information about who the data is shared with;
- withdrawal of consent, when the processing is based on it;
- objection to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise your rights, contact us at the email above. You may also file a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD).
