## AI Summarizer

**AI Summarizer** is a Google Chrome extension that uses AI to summarize only the part of the page you choose.

### How it works

1. Open the extension's side panel.
2. Point to the content you want to summarize with a picker similar to the DevTools one: an article, a section or a post, without the menus, ads and other parts of the page.
3. The selected text goes to the API, which returns a short summary in Portuguese or English, with a main sentence and the key points.

### Why it's different

Since only the selected part is sent, the AI processes less text. The summary is more accurate and each use costs less.
