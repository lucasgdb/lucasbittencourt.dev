## AI Forward Deployed Engineer

An **AI Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** is a specialized software engineer embedded directly with clients or internal product groups to build, customize, and deploy real-world AI and agentic systems.

In practice, this means:

- understanding the process, the data and the systems the AI needs to use;
- building and customizing AI and agent solutions for that context;
- integrating them into the existing systems;
- deploying them to production and following up on the results.

### What I've built

AI systems of my own, in production, that show how I work:

- **[AI Summarizer](/tool/ai-summarizer)**: a browser extension, published in the Chrome Web Store, that summarizes the part of a page or PDF you pick with AI, turns summaries into audio and video, and fills in forms. It picks the most economical model per request, shows the price before charging and takes payments via Pix.
- **[The MCP server of this website](/mcp)**: AI assistants (Claude, Cursor, VS Code and others) connect to it with the Model Context Protocol and search the blog with ranked, cited passages (RAG over the posts).
- **[Video Downloader](/tool/video-downloader)**: a browser extension that finds the videos on a page, including HLS and DASH streams, and downloads them inside the browser.

The [blog](/blog) shows how I explain technical decisions, from the [Node.js event loop](/blog/nodejs-event-loop) to [React performance](/blog/react-performance).
