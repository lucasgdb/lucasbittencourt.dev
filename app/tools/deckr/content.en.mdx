**Deckr** is a free Clash Royale deck builder that runs in the browser, at [deckr.lucasbittencourt.dev](https://deckr.lucasbittencourt.dev/en). It rolls 8-card decks from the cards you choose, shows the average elixir cost and the 4-card cycle, and opens the deck straight in the game. It works without an account, in English and Portuguese.

## What it does

- **Roll a deck** from your card pool: every card, only those unlocked up to an arena, or your own pick.
- **Smart roll**: decks built around meta archetypes (Hog Cycle, Log Bait, Golem, LavaLoon, X-Bow and more), with a win condition, support, defense, spells and cycle. Each group shows in a square that explains what its cards are for.
- **Build card by card**, with search by name and filters by elixir and arena, drag cards to reorder them and lock the ones a new roll should keep.
- **Open in Clash Royale**, copy the deck link or share it from your phone. You can also paste a deck link from the game.
- **Save decks** in this browser, or in your account if you log in.

## Log in with Clash Royale (optional)

With your player tag and an official Clash Royale API key, Deckr shows your **profile** (trophies, win rate, King level), **current deck**, **collection**, **recent battles** (with each opponent's deck, ready to try) and **upcoming chests**. Your collection can become your card pool, so rolls only use the cards you have.

The key is free at [developer.clashroyale.com](https://developer.clashroyale.com/); Deckr shows the steps and the IP address to allow on the key.

## Privacy

Without logging in, everything stays in your browser. When you log in, Deckr keeps your tag, your profile data and your API key **encrypted**, and uses no advertising cookies or analytics. The details are in the [Privacy policy](/tool/deckr/privacy).

Deckr is a fan project, not official or endorsed by Supercell; game data comes from the official Clash Royale API, under [Supercell's Fan Content Policy](https://supercell.com/en/fan-content-policy/).
