AI Summarizer is now in the **Chrome Web Store**. In Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi, install it from there: updates arrive on their own. In **Firefox**, until it reaches Mozilla's store, it's installed by hand.

Pick your browser below; if it can be recognized, its tab is already open. It only works on a computer.

## Step by step

<Tabs group="browser">
<Tab label="Chrome" value="chrome">

1. Open [AI Summarizer's page in the Chrome Web Store](https://ai-summarizer.lucasbittencourt.dev/).
2. Click **Add to Chrome** and confirm with **Add extension**.
3. **Pin** the extension: click the puzzle icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to AI Summarizer.
4. Click its icon: the **side panel** opens. Sign in with your Google account.

It needs Chrome 116 or newer.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Edge" value="edge">

1. Open [AI Summarizer's page in the Chrome Web Store](https://ai-summarizer.lucasbittencourt.dev/).
2. In the bar at the top of the page, click **Allow extensions from other stores** and confirm with **Allow**. Only the first time.
3. Click the button to add the extension and confirm with **Add extension**.
4. **Show** the extension in the toolbar: click the puzzle icon (Extensions), then the eye icon next to AI Summarizer.
5. Click its icon: the **side panel** opens. Sign in with your Google account.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Brave" value="brave">

1. Open [AI Summarizer's page in the Chrome Web Store](https://ai-summarizer.lucasbittencourt.dev/).
2. Click the button to add the extension and confirm with **Add extension**.
3. **Pin** the extension: click the puzzle icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to AI Summarizer.
4. Click its icon: the **side panel** opens. Sign in with your Google account.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Opera" value="opera">

1. Opera needs an add-on to install extensions from the Chrome Web Store: install [Install Chrome Extensions](https://addons.opera.com/extensions/details/install-chrome-extensions/), from Opera's own store. Only the first time.
2. Open [AI Summarizer's page in the Chrome Web Store](https://ai-summarizer.lucasbittencourt.dev/).
3. Click the button to add the extension and confirm.
4. **Pin** the extension: click the cube icon (Extensions) next to the address bar, then the pin next to AI Summarizer.
5. Click its icon: the extension opens in a **popup**. Sign in with your Google account.

Opera doesn't have Chrome's side panel. So when you click **Select content** or **Select form**, the extension opens in a narrow window at the browser's right edge, which stays open while you click on the page.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Vivaldi" value="vivaldi">

1. Open [AI Summarizer's page in the Chrome Web Store](https://ai-summarizer.lucasbittencourt.dev/).
2. Click the button to add the extension and confirm with **Add extension**.
3. The extension's icon shows up in the toolbar (or under the Extensions button, next to the address bar).
4. Click its icon: the **side panel** opens. Sign in with your Google account.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Firefox" value="firefox">

<DownloadBuild browser="firefox" />

1. Type `about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox` in the address bar and press Enter.
2. Click **Load Temporary Add-on…** and pick the downloaded `.zip` file, without unzipping it.
3. Click the puzzle icon (Extensions), then AI Summarizer. To keep it in the toolbar, use the gear next to its name and **Pin to Toolbar**.
4. Click its icon: Firefox's **sidebar** opens with the extension. Sign in with your Google account.

It needs Firefox 128 or newer. **The install is temporary**: Firefox only keeps extensions signed by Mozilla for good, so this one goes away when you close the browser, and you have to load it again. The signed version, which stays installed, comes later.

</Tab>

## Testing

To check that everything works:

1. **Sign in**: your name, picture and credit balance show up. Every new account gets welcome credits.
2. **Summarize**: open any article, click **Select content**, point at the text and click it. The price shows on the **Summarize** button; confirm and watch the summary being written.
3. **Listen and watch**: on the finished summary, generate the audio and the video. The video takes a few minutes.
4. **Fill**: open a page with a form, go to **fill forms**, click **Select a form**, write how you want to answer it and click **Fill**. Check the answers before submitting; **Undo** puts back what was there.
5. **History**: the summary is there, with its translations, audio and video.

If something fails, send me the browser, its version and what happened at the support email, in the links above.

## Updating

- **Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera and Vivaldi**: the store updates the extension on its own.
- **Firefox**: new versions show up on this page. Load the new `.zip` as a temporary add-on, as when installing. Your account, credits and history are on the server.

## When it reaches Firefox's store

Install it from the store and sign in with the same Google account: everything will be there.
