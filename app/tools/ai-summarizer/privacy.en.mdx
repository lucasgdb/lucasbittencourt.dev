## 2. What the extension does

AI Summarizer uses artificial intelligence to summarize the content you choose: part of a page, the whole page or a PDF. Summaries are kept in your history, can be translated into other languages and are paid for with credits. It can also **fill in forms**: you pick a form and say how you want to answer it, and the AI fills in the fields for you to review and submit.

The extension **only reads a page when you ask**, by clicking "Select content", "Use the whole page", "Read PDF" or "Select a form". It never submits a form for you. It doesn't read or send anything from the pages you just visit.

## 3. Data we process

### Account

To use the extension, you sign in with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by the **account service** shared by my tools (lb-auth-api.lucasbittencourt.dev), so the same account works in AI Summarizer, [Video Downloader](/tool/video-downloader) and future tools. From Google we receive only your **name**, **email**, **profile picture** and **account identifier**, which the account service keeps to identify you. We have no access to your Gmail, your Drive or any other data in your Google account.

The AI Summarizer server keeps only what belongs to this extension (your credit balance, history and purchases), linked to your account's internal identifier. It doesn't receive your name or email, and the other tools don't receive your AI Summarizer data.

Signing in creates a session in the account service, valid for this extension. The extension keeps the session code in your browser; the account service keeps only an encrypted version of it (a hash). With each request, the AI Summarizer server asks the account service whose session it is.

### Content you choose to summarize

When you ask for a summary, we send the server:

- the chosen **text** (the selected part, the page's text or the text extracted from the PDF), with its structure as Markdown (headings, lists, tables and code blocks; only the text of links), up to 60,000 characters;
- the page's **title** and **address (URL)**;
- the summary's **options**: model, style, length, effort, language and any preferences you write.

This content is sent to work out the price (the quote) and to make the summary, which is generated by an artificial intelligence provider (see section 6). **The page's text isn't stored** by the server, only its number of characters. For a PDF, the file is downloaded and read by your browser and only the extracted text is sent.

### Forms you choose to fill

When you ask to fill a form, we send the server:

- what you wrote about **how to answer** it (it may include personal data, if you write any);
- for each field: its **label**, help text, options, type and limits, whether it's required and whether it's already answered. **What you've already typed into the fields isn't sent**;
- the page's **title** and **address (URL)**, and the options you chose.

**Password, payment card, file, one-time code and hidden fields are never read.** This content is sent to work out the price and to generate the answers, using the same artificial intelligence providers as the summaries (see section 6). The extension writes the answers into the page, and "Undo" puts back what was there. **Nothing about the form is stored**: it doesn't go to the history, and what you wrote isn't saved, neither on the server nor in your browser. Only the charge is recorded in the credit statement.

### Summaries, translations and history

We keep what the extension makes for you, so it stays in your history:

- the summary (title, preview and text) and the translations you ask for;
- the language, the options used, the model, the credits spent and how much text was processed (tokens);
- the title, address and site of the source page, the type of content (page, selection, PDF or text file) and the date.

### Credits

We keep your balance and the statement of movements: welcome credits, purchases, summaries, translations, form fills, refunds and adjustments, with the amount, the balance after each one and the title of the related summary.

### Credit purchases

Purchases are paid via Pix (Brazilian instant payment) and processed by **AbacatePay**. To create the charge, we send it only the **amount**, the **description** (number of credits and bonus) and an **internal purchase ID**. **We don't send your name, email or CPF.** As with any Pix payment, AbacatePay and the institutions involved receive from your bank the payer data required by the Pix rules.

For each purchase we keep the pack, the credits and bonus, the amount, the payment status, the Pix code and the creation, expiration and payment dates. We have no access to your bank account data.

### Data kept in your browser

The extension keeps in your browser's local storage the session (with name, email, picture and balance), the interface language, the theme and your last summary options. This data isn't sent to anyone, except the session code, which goes with each request to the AI Summarizer server and the account service.

The page information shown on the start screen (title, description, word count and language) is worked out in your browser and isn't sent to our server. To show the site's icon, the extension uses Chrome's cache and may request the site's own `/favicon.ico`; that request goes from your browser straight to the site, like a normal visit, without going through our server.

### Technical logs

The hosting infrastructure records technical data about requests, such as IP address, date and time and the address called, for security and operation. The server logs errors and usage events (such as the account ID, the model used and purchases), but doesn't log the text of pages.

### What we don't collect

The extension doesn't collect your browsing history, the content of pages you didn't ask to summarize or fill, what you type into forms, passwords or card data, and doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising tracking.

## 4. Extension permissions

- **activeTab** and **scripting**: read the open tab, only when you click one of the actions, and write the answers into the form fields when you ask to fill one.
- **Access to sites** (optional): requested only if you click "Allow access", to read the page even after navigating. Even with this access, the extension only reads a page when you ask.
- **sidePanel**: show the extension in the side panel.
- **storage**: keep the session and preferences in your browser.
- **identity**: open Google's sign-in window.
- **favicon**: show the site's icon from Chrome's cache.
- **Access to the extension's server**: send the summary, translation, form fill, history and credit requests.
- The **account service** (sign-in, your name and picture, signing out) answers the extension without any extra permission.

## 6. Sharing

We don't sell personal data, don't use it for advertising and don't pass it on to data brokers. Data is shared only with:

- **OpenAI**: generates the summaries, translations and form answers from the content sent, when you use an OpenAI model (the default). The content is sent without being stored in OpenAI's history, and OpenAI's API terms don't use this data to train models.
- **DeepSeek**: generates the summary or the form answers only if you choose a DeepSeek model, when one is available in the model list. In that case, the content is processed under DeepSeek's terms and privacy policy.
- **Railway**: hosting of the servers (the extension's and the account service) and the database.
- **Google**: sign-in with the Google account.
- **AbacatePay**: processing of Pix payments.
- **Public authorities**: when required by law or court order.

## 7. Limited use

The use of information received by the extension follows the [Chrome Web Store User Data Policy](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/webstore/program-policies/user-data-faq), including the **Limited Use** requirements: data is used only to provide and improve the extension's single purpose (summarizing the content you choose and filling in the forms you choose with AI), isn't transferred for advertising or to determine creditworthiness, and isn't read by people, except with your consent, for security and abuse investigation, or when required by law.

## 8. International transfer

OpenAI, Railway (servers in the United States) and Google store or process data outside Brazil, mainly in the United States. If you choose a DeepSeek model, the content may be processed in China, under DeepSeek's policy. These transfers follow article 33 of the LGPD, with providers that adopt security and data protection measures.

## 9. How long we keep data

- **Summaries and translations**: until you delete them. Deleting a summary erases its text and that of its translations. Deleting a single translation erases only that one.
- **Credits and usage records** (statement, credits spent, amount of text processed and dates): while the account exists, and then for the periods required by tax law.
- **Account**: until you ask for it to be deleted. The account is shared by my tools; you can ask to delete only the AI Summarizer data or the whole account.
- **Session**: valid for up to 30 days. It's deleted when you sign out, and expired sessions are deleted automatically. "Sign out of all devices" ends your sessions in every tool.
- **Purchases**: for the periods required by tax law.
- **Access logs**: for the 6 months required by article 15 of the Brazilian Internet Civil Framework (Marco Civil da Internet).
- **Data in your browser**: until you sign out or uninstall the extension.

After that, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 10. Your rights

Under article 18 of the LGPD, you may ask for confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent.

You can delete summaries and translations directly in the extension's history. To delete your account or exercise other rights, write to the email above. You can also revoke the extension's access to your Google account at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) and file a complaint with the Brazilian Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

## 11. Security

All communication with the servers is encrypted (HTTPS), sessions are stored encrypted (hashed), the servers talk to each other over a private network with their own credentials, and access to data is restricted. Page content is treated as untrusted: it's never shown as code, and the AI provider is instructed to ignore commands in it. No system is completely immune to incidents; if a relevant incident occurs, the affected data subjects and the ANPD will be notified, as required by law.
