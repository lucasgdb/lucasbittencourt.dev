**AI Summarizer** is a browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox) that uses AI to summarize only the content you choose. With menus, ads and comments left out, the AI processes less text: the summary comes out more accurate and costs less. Then you can **listen** to the summary or **watch** it as a video. The extension also **fills in forms** with the answers you point it to.

## What it can summarize

- **Part of a page**: point at the article, section or post with a picker like the DevTools one.
- **The whole page**: the page's main content, in one go.
- **PDFs** up to 30 MB: every page or just one, read in the browser itself.
- **Text and code files** opened in the browser, such as `.txt`, `.md` and source code.

The content goes to the AI with its structure kept (headings, lists, tables and code blocks), up to 60,000 characters per summary.

## How it works

1. Click the extension's icon to open its side panel (or, if you prefer, a popup from the toolbar) and sign in with your Google account. It's the same account as in my other tools, such as [Video Downloader](/tool/video-downloader).
2. Choose the content: a selection, the whole page or the PDF.
3. Adjust the options if you like: style (summary, bullet points, detailed, simple or study notes), length, effort, language and free-text preferences, such as "focus on the numbers". The AI model is picked automatically: the most economical one for each request.
4. See the price before confirming, and watch the summary being written, already formatted. If you close the panel, the summary keeps being generated and lands in your history.

## Features

- **History** with search and filters by type, site and period.
- Summaries in **10 languages**: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Hindi.
- **Translations** of a summary into other languages, without summarizing the page again.
- **Export** the summary as Markdown (`.md`) and copy the text.
- **Listen to the summary**: the summary (or a translation) becomes narrated audio, with speed from 1× to 2×, which you can also download as MP3. Available in English and Portuguese.
- **Watch it as a video**: the summary becomes a narrated presentation, with slides that appear along with the narration, in 720p, to watch full screen or download as MP4. It takes a few minutes to be ready; also in English and Portuguese.
- **Fill in forms**: pick a form (or use the whole page) and say how you want to answer it, with the tone, answer length and language. The AI fills in the fields, you review and submit. It never submits for you, never makes up personal details you didn't write, "Undo" puts back what was there, and password and card fields are left out.
- Interface in **English** and **Portuguese**, with light and dark themes, in the side panel or a popup.

## Credits

- Every account gets **welcome credits** the first time it uses the extension.
- If the AI fails, the credits come back automatically; the same goes for an audio or video that doesn't get made. In a form fill, once the answers start coming in, the credits stay charged, even if you stop it partway. Asking again for a translation that already exists is free.
- An audio or video already made is kept with the summary: opening it again is free.
- Deleting a summary from the history doesn't give back the credits spent on it, or on its audio and videos.
