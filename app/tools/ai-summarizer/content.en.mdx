**AI Summarizer** is a Google Chrome extension that uses AI to summarize only the content you choose. With menus, ads and comments left out, the AI processes less text: the summary comes out more accurate and costs less.

## What it can summarize

- **Part of a page**: point at the article, section or post with a picker like the DevTools one.
- **The whole page**: the page's main content, in one go.
- **PDFs** up to 30 MB: every page or just one, read in the browser itself.
- **Text and code files** opened in Chrome, such as `.txt`, `.md` and source code.

The content goes to the AI with its structure kept (headings, lists, tables and code blocks), up to 60,000 characters per summary.

## How it works

1. Open the extension's side panel and sign in with your Google account. It's the same account as in my other tools, such as [Video Downloader](/tool/video-downloader).
2. Choose the content: a selection, the whole page or the PDF.
3. Adjust the options if you like: model, style (summary, bullet points, detailed, simple or study notes), length, effort, language and free-text preferences, such as "focus on the numbers".
4. See the price before confirming, and watch the summary being written, already formatted. If you close the panel, the summary keeps being generated and lands in your history.

## Features

- **History** with search and filters by type, site, model, style and period.
- Summaries in **10 languages**: English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese, Japanese and Hindi.
- **Translations** of a summary into other languages, without summarizing the page again.
- **Export** the summary as Markdown (`.md`) and copy the text.
- **Fill in forms**: pick a form and say how you want to answer it; the AI fills in the fields, you review and submit. It never submits for you, "Undo" puts back what was there, and password and card fields are left out.
- Interface in **English** and **Portuguese**, with light and dark themes.

## Credits

- Each summary, translation and form fill uses credits, depending on the content's size and the model, effort, style and length chosen. The price shows up before you confirm, and it's exactly what's charged.

- Deleting a summary from the history doesn't give back the credits spent on it.
