**4People** brings together more than 100 free online tools for development, study and work, at [4people.lucasbittencourt.dev](https://4people.lucasbittencourt.dev/). Everything runs in the browser: what you type isn't sent to any server. It works without an account and is in beta; the app is in Portuguese.

## What it does

- **Generators and validators** for Brazilian documents (CPF, CNPJ, RG, CNH, PIS, RENAVAM, voter ID), license plates, test credit cards (with a custom BIN and CSV and JSON export), passwords, UUIDs, names and fictional people.
- **Development**: JWT generator and decoder, regex, XPath and JSONPath testers, a REST API tester, timestamp converter, cron describer and text diff.
- **Converters**: JSON, YAML, XML, INI and CSV to SQL, Base64, URL, HTML, number bases, colors, units (length, mass, temperature, area, volume and more) and live **currency exchange rates**.
- **Networking**: IPv4 and IPv6 subnet calculators, SLA, download time, my IP and Brazilian postal code (CEP) lookup.
- **Math**: percentages, rule of three, compound interest, quadratic equations, GCD and LCM, Roman numerals with no upper limit, areas and date calculations.
- **Installable** as an app (PWA), and pages already opened work offline.

## Privacy

Calculations happen on your device. There's no account, no ads, no cookies and no visit statistics. Only a few tools query public services, such as postal codes, banks, IP and exchange rates. The details are in the [Privacy policy](/tool/4people/privacy) and the [End user license agreement (EULA)](/tool/4people/eula), and the [source code](https://github.com/lucasgdb/4People) is public.
