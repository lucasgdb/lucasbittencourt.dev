**Cheatsheets** is a free tool with searchable cheat sheets for the command line, at [cheatsheet.lucasbittencourt.dev](https://cheatsheet.lucasbittencourt.dev/). Each cheat sheet puts every shortcut and command for a tool on one screen, without ads. It works without an account; the app is in English.

## What it does

- **tmux**: more than 80 shortcuts and commands for sessions, windows, panes, copy mode and config. Pick your prefix key (`Ctrl+b`, `Ctrl+a` or `Ctrl+Space`) and every shortcut updates.
- **kubectl**: nearly 100 everyday commands for setup and completion, contexts, pods, logs and debugging, deployments and rollouts, jobs, manifests, RBAC, nodes and secrets, plus the short names of each resource. Switch to the `k` alias and every command updates.
- **Search** across titles, shortcuts and commands, with `/` to jump to the search field.
- **One click to copy** any command, and **print** a clean two-column version with your prefix or alias applied.
- **Links to each section** (like `/tmux#panes`), and each cheat sheet shows when it was last checked and against which official docs.
- **Status bar** in the style of tmux, to jump to each section.
- Each cheat sheet has **a look of its own**, with the colors of the tool it covers.

## Privacy

What you search and copy never leaves your browser. There's no account, no ads, no cookies and no usage statistics: only your prefix key and alias stay on your device. The details are in the [Privacy policy](/tool/cheatsheets/privacy).
