## 2. What Cheatsheets does

Cheatsheets shows the shortcuts and commands of command line tools (tmux and kubectl), with search, a configurable prefix key or alias and one-click copy. **Everything happens in the browser itself**: there's no account, no login and no server that receives what you search or copy.

## 3. Data we process

### Searches and copied commands

What you type in the search field is used only to filter the page on your device. **It isn't sent, logged or kept**. When you click a command, it's written to your device's clipboard; nothing is sent to a server.

### Preferences in your browser

So you don't have to pick them again on every visit, your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) keeps the tmux prefix key and the kubectl alias you chose. They stay only on your device and aren't sent to servers.

### Usage statistics

Cheatsheets **uses no usage statistics or analytics** tools.

### Hosting logs

Vercel, which hosts the site, records technical data about requests (such as IP address, date and time and the address requested) in its platform logs, for its retention period, for operations and security.

## 4. Cookies

Cheatsheets **uses no cookies**: no session, advertising or analytics cookies.

Vercel processes data on servers outside Brazil, including in the United States. These transfers follow article 33 of the LGPD, with a provider that adopts security and data protection measures.

- **Preferences in your browser**: until you delete them, by clearing the site's data in your browser settings.
- **Hosting logs**: for Vercel's retention period.

## 9. Your rights

Under article 18 of the LGPD, you may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, anonymization, blocking or deletion, portability, information about sharing and withdrawal of consent. Since what you search and copy never reaches a server, there's no data of yours kept to look up or delete.

To exercise these rights, write to the email above. You can also file a complaint with Brazil's National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

## 10. Security

All communication is encrypted (HTTPS), and what you search and copy never leaves your device. The fonts are served by the site itself, so loading the page sends no requests to third parties.
