**Video Downloader** is a browser extension (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and Firefox) that finds the videos on the page you're on and downloads them. Everything happens inside your browser: the videos go straight from the site to your computer, without passing through any server of mine.

## What it finds

- **Video files** played by the page, such as MP4 and WebM, and links to them.
- **Streams** in HLS (`.m3u8`) and DASH (`.mpd`), which the extension downloads in pieces and assembles into a single MP4 file (MKV when the codecs don't fit), without re-encoding.
- **By link**: paste the address of a public X (Twitter) or Reddit post, or a direct link to a video file or playlist, and pick the quality.

Pieces of streams, audio tracks and thumbnails aren't listed as videos: each video shows up once, with its resolution, duration, size and the reason for its classification.

## How it works

1. Open the page with the video, open the extension (side panel or popup) and play the video: most players only load it on play, and it shows up by itself.
2. Allow access to sites when asked, so the extension can see the player's requests. It's optional and can be revoked in the browser at any time.
3. Choose the **quality** and the **audio track** when there's more than one, and click Download. You can pause, resume and cancel.

## Features

- Quality (resolution, frame rate, codec, bitrate) and audio track pickers, with the track's language.
- Every downloaded file is checked before being saved; the browser's download manager does the saving.
- **Copy URL** with the exact address the page used.
- Side panel or toolbar popup, interface in **English** and **Portuguese**, light and dark themes.

## Limits

- Videos protected by **DRM** or encryption and **live streams** aren't downloaded, on purpose.
- YouTube's own player uses a proprietary protocol and isn't supported.
- Only download videos you're allowed to download. Respecting copyright and each site's terms is up to you.
